One Fella. One Basement.

Dodge Designed This... Who?

Dodge Designed This is a one-person, DIY screen-printed punk fashion operation — born out of a Sheffield basement, a heat press, and a band that needed merch nobody else was going to make.

The Dodge Designed This studio — printed tees and Von Dodge trucker caps on display next to the heat press and screen printing rig
The actual setup. One room, one press, zero corporate nonsense.

Our Birth

Dodge Designed This started as a way to make merch for GTFOD when nobody else would print small runs at a price that made sense. One screen, one heat press, and a lot of trial and error turned into an actual shop.

Collabs & Chaos

Along the way it's picked up a few collaborators — shoutout to Thomas Mannion and Tokyo-based kiYOmi for lending their art and ideas to some of the prints you'll see on the rail.

DIY Printing, Every Time

Every single piece is hand screen-printed, one at a time. No outsourcing, no print-on-demand factory somewhere else in the world — just ink, mesh and a lot of patience.

Ink buckets, tape and cassettes on the print bench

What You Get

Free UK postage on everything, no exceptions. What you see is what gets made — small batches, so once it's gone, it might not come back in quite the same way.

Sizes & Custom Orders

Sizing runs XS–XL on most prints (2XL on request). Can't see your size on a product page? Just get in touch — custom runs are no bother, that's the whole point of doing this small.

The Dodge Ethos

Ink first, ask later. No two prints are ever quite identical, and that's not a flaw — it's the whole point. Homage, never theft. Small runs, made to be worn and wrecked, not sat in a drawer.
